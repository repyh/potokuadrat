# Seed photographs

The development seed album uses photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)
so the gallery, lightbox, downloads, and zip look like real client work without a
Google Drive key. The list lives in `src/server/drive/seed-photos.ts`; the media
routes crop each photo to the seeded dimensions (3:2 landscape, 2:3 portrait) and
cache the result under `.cache/seed/`.

All photographs are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license):
free to use, no permission or attribution required. Credits are listed as a courtesy.
